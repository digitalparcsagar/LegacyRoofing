Website Design
Strategy.

Your website is often the first impression homeowners have of your company. Before someone picks up the phone or requests an estimate, they've already formed an opinion based on your online presence.

Legacy Roofing has built an excellent reputation through quality workmanship and customer service. Our goal is to ensure the website reflects that same level of professionalism. Rather than simply updating colors and layouts, we will redesign the entire experience around one objective:

Turning more website visitors into qualified
roofing opportunities.

Our Design Approach
01
Discover
We begin by understanding Legacy Roofing's business, customers, services, competitive advantages, and sales process. This allows us to create a website that accurately represents your company while supporting your business goals.
02
Design
We create a completely custom website tailored specifically to Legacy Roofing. Every page is thoughtfully designed to build credibility, answer customer questions, and guide visitors toward requesting an estimate.
03
Develop
Once designs are approved, we build a responsive website that performs beautifully across desktop, tablet, and mobile devices. Performance, speed, usability, and accessibility remain priorities throughout development.
04
Launch
Before launch, every page is thoroughly tested. We verify navigation, forms, mobile responsiveness, page speed, browser compatibility, and overall user experience to ensure a smooth transition.

Our Plan of Attack.

01
Project Discovery

Planning session covering goals, customers, services, competitors, and user experience. Deliverables: website objectives, sitemap, content organization, user journey planning.

02
Creative Direction

Defining typography, colors, imagery, page layouts, and iconography to create a premium appearance that reinforces trust and stands apart from competitors.

03
Custom Page Design

Each page individually designed around its purpose, including: Homepage, About, Residential, Commercial, Storm Damage, Roof Repair, Gallery, Financing, and Contact.

04
Responsive Development

Every page developed to function flawlessly across desktop, tablet, and mobile. The experience remains consistent regardless of screen size or device.

05
Quality Assurance

Thorough pre-launch testing of mobile responsiveness, contact forms, page speed, browser compatibility, navigation, internal links, and overall usability.

06
Website Launch

Final approval, launch, and post-launch testing. Everything transitions smoothly with zero disruption to your business or existing traffic.
